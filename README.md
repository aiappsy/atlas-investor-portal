# ATLAS Travel Club - Institutional Investor Portal & Data Room

A dedicated, institutional-grade investor application and confidential data room for **ATLAS Travel Club LLC**.

## Overview
ATLAS connects frequent travelers directly to institutional B2B hotel wholesale liquidity behind a verified, password-gated membership. While public retail Online Travel Agencies (OTAs) add 20% to 35% in commission markups, ATLAS passes 100% of wholesale savings directly to members at **0% retail markup** (saving $300 to $600+ on everyday 4-star and 5-star hotel stays). ATLAS monetizes via predictable **96% gross margin software subscription ARR** ($799–$1,799/yr) and secondary payment interchange.
